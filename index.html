Privacy Policy

Here's how we handle your information, in plain language.

Your rights
You can request a copy of what we have on file, ask how it's been used, or close your account at any time. Closing deletes your identity information per our retention policy.
How your information is used
We use your information only to verify your identity, secure your account, and meet legal reporting requirements. We never sell your information to advertisers or other companies.
What does encryption mean?
Encryption scrambles your information so only someone with the right key can read it. Even if it were intercepted, it would look like meaningless text. We encrypt your data both while it's traveling to us and while it's stored.
Data retention & deletion
We keep your information only as long as your account is open, or as long as the law requires for tax and financial records. Closing your account starts the deletion process.
